Cut Trainer Live

A first test of live AR at a real table: can your phone find the table and keep marks pinned to the balls while you move?

  1. Set up a cue ball and an object ball on the table.
  2. Press Start AR, then slowly sweep the phone over the table until the crosshair turns solid.
  3. Aim the crosshair at the centre of the cue ball and press Mark. Then the object ball, then the middle of the pocket.
  4. Call the fraction. The ghost ball, aim line and path appear on the table.
  5. Walk around the shot: the marks should stay on the balls. If they slide, note where and how much.
